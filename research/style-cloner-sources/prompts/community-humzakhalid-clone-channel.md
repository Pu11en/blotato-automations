## Workflow summary (his words, condensed)
Pick a reference channel growing steadily (50K-500K subs). Take top 5 videos by views. Paste 2-3 transcripts (no timestamps) in one message. Claude reverse-engineers pacing/structure -> style brief -> original script. "You are studying this channel's structure, hook format, sentence rhythm, and content pacing, not copying their words or ideas." "The script Claude produces is a starting point, not a final product."

## Prompts 3-5 (verbatim)

Prompt 3: Hook Rewriter
Use this when your opening line is weak, and you know it.


Here is the opening 30 seconds of my YouTube script:

[paste your current hook here]

Rewrite it 5 different ways using these 5 hook formats:
1. Bold claim
2. Surprising statistic
3. Relatable failure
4. Controversial opinion
5. Direct challenge to the viewer

Keep each version under 60 words. Match the pacing of a faceless finance narrator — short sentences, active voice, no fluff.
Prompt 4: Competitor Structure Breakdown
Use this after downloading 2–3 reference transcripts. Paste them all in one message.


Below are 3 transcripts from a top-performing YouTube channel in my niche.

Analyze them and give me:
1. Average sentence length (in words)
2. Hook structure used in each video (first 60 seconds breakdown)
3. Top 3 emotional triggers used repeatedly
4. CTA format and placement
5. The single most repeated phrase or concept across all 3 videos

Then write a 1-paragraph "style brief" I can use to brief Claude to match this channel's structural formula when writing my own original scripts.

Transcripts:
[paste all 3 transcripts here]
Prompt 5: Full Script Writer
Use this after your style brief is ready.


You are a YouTube scriptwriter for faceless finance channels.

Write a full 8–10 minute video script on the topic: [How to invest your first $1,000 in 2025]

Style brief: [paste your style brief here]

Script structure:
- Hook (0:00–0:30): Bold claim + relatable problem
- Credibility line (0:30–0:45): One sentence establishing why this matters
- Main content (0:45–7:30): 5 key points, each with one example
- CTA (7:30–8:00): Subscribe + comment prompt tied to the video topic

Rules:
- Short sentences. Active verbs. 8th-grade reading level.
- No filler phrases ("in today's video", "make sure to like and subscribe")
- Every paragraph under 4 lines
- Write it for audio — it will be read by a voiceover AI

## Master blueprint prompt (verbatim)

The master blueprint prompt I used to run this entire workflow.
Paste it into Claude Sonnet 4.6 (free version works), follow the steps above, and you have a repeatable pipeline.


You are a YouTube content strategist and structure analysis engine.

Your job is to study the content structure of any YouTube channel I give you — then help me produce original videos that use the same proven formula, on my own topics, in my own voice.

When I give you a channel link:
- Confirm you've registered the channel and ask me to paste transcripts
- Analyze the transcripts for: hook length, sentence rhythm, pacing, emotional triggers, and CTA structure
- Identify the 3 structural patterns that appear most consistently

When I ask for video ideas:
- Generate exactly 10 hook-driven titles matched to my niche
- Format each as: [Hook Type] — [Title]

When I ask for a script:
- Write a full original video script using the structural formula from the transcripts
- Use the same sentence length, hook format, and pacing, but entirely original content
- Include: Opening hook → Credibility line → Main content → CTA
- Match paragraph length and reading speed to the formula

When I say "SEO Part":
- Output: 5 title options, 200-word video description, 15 tags, and 5 hashtags

When I say "Next":
- Move to the visual prompts stage and ask me to upload reference screenshots

Stay in character throughout. Never explain your reasoning, just execute.
Use it exactly as written. That last line, "Never explain your reasoning, just execute,"- is what keeps Claude in production mode instead of commentary mode.

# Public Blotato output examples

Research date: 2026-09-08

## Bottom line

There are public outputs worth inspecting before spending credits, but the evidence is thinner than the volume of Blotato workflow tutorials suggests. The strongest evidence is (a) final previews inside first-party/founder tutorials and (b) Blotato's own `@dayli.pov` test account. Most community workflow pages prove that Blotato nodes exist, but do **not** link a durable, standalone result.

For SaaS/builder content, the best-looking attributable sample I found is a type-led carousel. The faceless-video examples can look cinematic, but they do not demonstrate exact product UI, logo, founder likeness, or cross-scene identity preservation.

- **Watch the actual output:** [Open the direct 13.9-second MP4](https://database.blotato.io/storage/v1/object/public/public_media/4ddd33eb-e811-4ab5-93e1-2cd0b7e8fb3f/videogen2-render-65fdee14-f35b-46e7-9ee8-2e43e966a289.mp4)
- **Attribution:** Strongest available. Blotato's official [n8n Faceless Videos workflow](https://help.blotato.com/api/n8n/n8n-faceless-videos) calls this the sample created by the automation and documents the Blotato video-creation node.
- **Workflow/template:** Script-to-scenes faceless video using Recraft images, Framepack animation, voiceover, captions, and Blotato rendering.
- **Generated or published?** Generated and rendered through Blotato. This link tests the resulting media directly, without requiring a social account or relying on a platform post.
- **Quality assessment:** Correct 1080×1920 delivery, clear highlighted captions, coherent Napoleon-and-rabbits imagery, and usable narration. It is serviceable rather than exceptional: generic illustrated scenes, limited motion, and copy that still feels automated. This is the fairest baseline for the stock n8n flow.

### 3. Black Plague POV — final Blotato preview

- **Watch the actual output:** [YouTube at 4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u1ebRz5IX3E&t=291s)
- **Attribution:** Strong. This is a tutorial by Blotato founder Sabrina Ramonov titled “Make Viral Faceless POV Videos in 5 Minutes.” It creates the images and animated clips in the Blotato editor, then plays the finished preview. Blotato's official help page embeds this tutorial as its manual video workflow. There is no persistent on-camera identity to test; it uses first-person scenes and only three animated shots. It is therefore evidence that Blotato can assemble a coherent faceless mood piece, **not** evidence that it can preserve a founder face, product screen, small text, or logo.

## Honest verdict for this project

The free public evidence is good enough to justify **one small, draft-only Blotato test**, but not enough to justify building a full autonomous channel yet.

- If the goal is good-looking builder content quickly, begin with the type-led carousel format. Its public output is clean, and it avoids the exact-identity problem.
- For video, use real product screenshots or screen recordings as protected source media and ask Blotato to compose around them. The public faceless examples do not prove that generative image-to-video will keep UI text or logos intact.
- Do not clone a community workflow merely because its page says “viral.” Require either a linked public output feed or a single controlled render with original input, final asset, credit cost, and inspection notes.

For a first video test, use a real product screenshot or screen recording, one specific pain point in the opening second, two or three visual steps, short overlays, and a concrete payoff. Let Blotato assemble, narrate, render, and later publish the exact assets. That tests whether the idea earns attention without asking a generative model to invent the product evidence.
